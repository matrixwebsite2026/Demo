Building Protection & Sustainable Performance

One system. Your roof stops leaking, your building runs cooler, and your paperwork is done for you.

Matrix builds a complete protective layer for roofs, walls, windows and floors — designed for Gulf conditions: intense sun, sudden downpours, and surfaces that expand and crack in the heat.

Why It Matters

Heat, water and surface performance affect every building.

Buildings in the GCC experience intense solar exposure, high temperatures and sudden weather conditions. These factors can increase heat gain, accelerate surface deterioration and create additional maintenance requirements.

A Matrix Cool Roof reflects heat away instead. Less heat in can mean less air-conditioning demand, lower electricity use and lower operational emissions.
